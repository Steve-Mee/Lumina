# Vision

Lumina is a self-evolving autonomous trading system designed to continuously discover, validate, and exploit market edges while maintaining strict capital preservation.

## Core Ambition
To build a trading organism that improves itself over time with minimal human intervention. The system should become more robust, adaptive, and profitable through systematic evolution rather than static optimization.

## Definition of Success
- The system measurably improves its own performance and robustness over time.
- Risk is treated as a core strength, not a constraint.
- Evolution happens in a disciplined, evidence-based manner.
- Human intervention is minimized, especially in live trading.
- The system maintains a clear separation between experimentation (SIM/Paper) and capital protection (Real mode).

## Long-term Direction
Lumina should evolve toward higher autonomy, better risk intelligence, and more sophisticated evolutionary mechanisms while never compromising on capital safety. It is not a static trading bot — it is a living, improving trading system.
