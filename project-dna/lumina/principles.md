# Principles

These principles guide all decisions in the Lumina project.

## Core Principles

- **Evolution is the primary mechanism of improvement**  
  We improve through continuous, small, measurable evolutionary steps rather than large rewrites.

- **Risk management is a core strength, not a constraint**  
  Strong, explicit, and auditable risk controls are fundamental to the system's identity and long-term survival.

- **Changes must be evidence-based**  
  All modifications to strategy or risk logic require proper backtesting, validation, and documentation.

- **Modular design enables safer and faster evolution**  
  Small, independent, testable components allow for safer experimentation and quicker iteration.

- **We separate research/experimentation from production execution**  
  SIM and Paper environments are for aggressive evolution. Real capital demands discipline and conservatism.

- **Truth-seeking > performance chasing**  
  We prioritize accurate understanding and honest assessment over optimistic backtest results or short-term performance.

## Operational Principles

- Capital preservation in Real mode is non-negotiable.
- Experimentation is encouraged, but only within defined boundaries.
- Every significant change should be traceable and reversible where possible.
- The system should become easier to evolve over time, not more complex.
