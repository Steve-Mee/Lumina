# 2026-05-29 — DNA Guardian v0.8.0 — Richer Recommendations

**Increment of**: DNA Validation & Scoring Tool

## What was implemented
- Strengthened the recommendation logic:
  - When the overall Health Score is declining, the recommendation now starts with "Attention: DNA Health Score is declining."
  - The normal human-readable report (`--report`) now always shows a clear "**Recommended Focus**" section.
  - Added guidance in the report: "Consider re-running the Guardian after addressing the focus area to measure improvement."
- Version bumped to 0.8.0.

## Why this step
Having a recommendation is good. Making it context-aware (especially sensitive to a declining trend) and consistently visible in everyday use makes the tool much more practical for day-to-day DNA health management.

This is a small but meaningful polish that increases the chance that the output is actually acted upon.

## Impact
The Guardian is becoming a more complete "coach" for the self-evolution of our Project DNA.
